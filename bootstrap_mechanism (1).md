## Part II: Correlation Length as Critical Parameter

### 2.1 Quantum Correlation Length

**In quantum field theory:**

Correlation function measures how field at point x correlates with field at point y:
$$C(x,y) = \langle \phi(x)\phi(y) \rangle$$

**Correlation length ξ:**

Distance over which correlations persist:
$$C(|x-y|) \sim e^{-|x-y|/\xi}$$

**For massive field with mass m:**
$$\xi = \frac{\hbar}{mc}$$

This is the Compton wavelength.

**For massless field (pure potential, no observation yet):**
$$\xi = \frac{\hbar c}{kT}$$

where T is temperature (thermal energy scale).

**Short correlation length (ξ << atomic scale):**

**Intermediate correlation length (ξ ~ atomic scale):**

- **Self-observation becomes possible**

**There's a Goldilocks zone.**
